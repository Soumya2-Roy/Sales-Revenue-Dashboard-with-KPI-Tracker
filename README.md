Sales Performance Dashboard & Analytics
Project Overview

This project is an Advanced Excel Sales Performance Dashboard developed to transform transaction-level sales data into a clear and structured business analytics solution.

The dashboard brings together key performance indicators, sales and profit trends, category analysis, state-level performance, customer profitability, and sub-category analysis in a single management-oriented view.

The project is designed to help users understand where sales are coming from, which areas generate profit, which customers contribute the most, and where profitability requires attention.

Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Track year-wise and month-wise sales trends.
Measure key business KPIs.
Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
Analyze state-level sales and profit performance.
Identify high-value and high-profit customers.
Analyze sub-category performance.
Create a clear Excel dashboard for management reporting.
Convert analytical findings into practical business recommendations.

Dashboard Analysis
1. Year-wise Performance

The dashboard compares sales and profit across the available years.

2023 recorded the highest annual sales:

$733,215.19

This analysis helps identify changes in overall business performance over time.

2. Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales are analyzed to identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.

Sales planning

Seasonal business decisions
3. Category Analysis

State-level analysis compares:

Customer-level analysis is used to identify customers contributing strongly to overall profit.

6. Sub-Category Analysis

Sub-category analysis provides a more detailed view within the major product categories.

Sales
Profit

This helps identify products or product groups that generate significant sales and/or profitability.

Key Insights

Based on the project analysis:

Total sales reached approximately $2.30 million.
Total profit was approximately $286.41 thousand.
The overall profit margin was approximately 12.5%.
2023 was the highest-sales year.
Technology was the highest-sales major category.
New Delhi recorded the highest state-level sales.
High-value customers make an important contribution to profitability.
Sales volume and profitability should be analyzed together because a high-sales segment may not always produce a high profit.

Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the project recommends focusing on:

1. Protecting Strong-Performing Categories

Continue monitoring Technology and other high-performing product groups.

2. Improving Low-Margin Areas

Review pricing, discounting, product mix, and other factors affecting categories with lower profitability.

3. Strengthening Customer Retention

Identify and retain customers with significant profit contributions.

4. Monitoring State-Level Profitability

Evaluate both sales and profit when comparing geographic markets.

5. Using Sales Trends for Planning

Use monthly and yearly patterns to support inventory and sales planning.
